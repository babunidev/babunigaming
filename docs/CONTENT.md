Use [BabuniGaming.webp](../assets/BabuniGaming.webp) as the portal image. This is the published 512 × 512 WebP of Babuni's cat artwork. The original drawing is credited to Babuni.

Add new recordings to [Videos](VIDEOS.md) in display order. Add dated accounts of creative decisions and AI collaboration to the [development journal](DEVLOG.md). Record actual changes in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).
