# Gaming and add-ons

Playing World of Warcraft gives me ideas for add-ons. Babuni Gaming is where I can share those experiences and show how some of the add-on ideas fit into the way I play.

With [Azeroth Moments](https://github.com/babunidev/azeroth-moments), I also have a project for capturing and sharing moments from games. The add-ons and Azeroth Moments each have their own project history.
